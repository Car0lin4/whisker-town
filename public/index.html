How to play
  • Pick one of your cats, then click the ground to walk.
  • Shift + click moves all your cats in this room.
  • Click any cat (yours or someone else's) to boop, play, groom, cuddle or pounce.
  • Walk to a signpost to go to another place.
  • Cats you leave alone will wander and make friends on their own.
